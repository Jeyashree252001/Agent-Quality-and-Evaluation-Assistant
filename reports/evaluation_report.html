Architectural Safeguards & Limitations of Model-Based Evaluation

In accordance with core requirements, an AI judge is never treated as unquestionable truth. Deterministic checks take absolute precedence:

  • No Overturn of Deterministic Rules: An AI judge cannot pass an agent that fails JSON Schema validation, tool selection contracts, or grounding bounds.
  • Mitigation of Verbosity Bias: LLM judges systematically favor longer, verbose responses regardless of actual factual fidelity. Deterministic token ratio bounds enforce conciseness.
  • Position & Self-Preference Drift: Model judges exhibit self-preference towards outputs resembling their own training data. Calibrated confidence thresholds force human escalation when scores fall into uncertainty zones (0.65 - 0.75).
  • Grounding over Fluency: Beautifully phrased hallucinations (such as assigning Mika to cost estimates when she merely asked for info) are caught by deterministic entity-commitment intersection, not left to model subjectivity.
